Again we offer unto thee this rational .... made perfect in faith: The priest or bishop takes the censer and says:

Exclamation: Especially for our most holy, most pure, most blessed and glorious Lady Theotokos and Ever-virgin Mary;

He censes before the holy table three times. Then the 2nd deacon censes around the holy table, returning the west side, censing it three times, and going to the SE corner censing the priest or bishop 3 times.

Meanwhile, the singers:1

It is truly meet to bless thee, O Theotokos, ever-blessed and most pure and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the cherubim and more glorious beyond compare than the seraphim, without corruption thou gavest birth to God the Word: true Theotokos, we magnify thee.

As they sing, the first deacon goes to the high place, makes the usual reverences, and kissing the altar as he passes, goes out through the Royal doors.

As the choir finishes the first Deacon standing in front of the Icon of Christ says:

FIRST DEACON For All Mankind!

CHOIR For All Mankind!

BISHOP Among the first ....

PRIEST Among the first ....

FIRST DEACON For His Eminence, The Most Reverend Michael, Archbishop of New York, of the Diocese of New York, and New Jersey, who here offers

The deacon now continues the prayer, while returning to the high place saying and pointing to the Diskos and Chalice as he passes:

These Holy Gifts

Continuing to the High Place

Unto the Lord our God

And reverencing the High Place and the Bishop continues:

For the most blessed Tikhon, Archbishop of Washington Metropolitan of all America and Canada.

And continuing returns to the arch of the Royal doors.

For the holy synod of Bishops, the Most Reverend Metropolitans, Archbishops and Bishops

For the honorable priesthood the diaconate in Christ

For every priestly and monastic order.

For this land, its civil authorities and armed forces.

For peace of the whole world For the good estate of God's Holy Churches

For the salvation and assistance of all those who diligently labor and serve

For the healing of the sick, for the repose, pardon, blessed memory, and forgiveness of sins of all Orthodox who have fallen asleep.

For the salvation of all the people here present, and for those they have in mind.

And for all mankind!

The First Deacon returns to the high place, makes the usual reverences, and receives a blessing from the Bishop, kissing His hand and shoulder saying:

Is Polla Eti Despota
