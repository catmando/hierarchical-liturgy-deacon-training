head: THE DIVINE LITURGY
folio: 136

Again we offer unto thee this rational worship for those who have fallen
asleep in the faith: ancestors, fathers, patriarchs, prophets, apostles,
preachers, evangelists, martyrs, confessors, ascetics, and every righteous
spirit made perfect in faith: {The priest takes the censer and says:}

{*Exclamation:*} Especially for our most holy, most pure, most blessed and
glorious Lady Theotokos and Ever-virgin Mary;

rubric: He censes before the holy table three times. Then the deacon censes
around the holy table, remembering whom he wishes from among the departed and
the living.

label: Meanwhile, the singers:[1]

small: It is truly meet to bless thee, O Theotokos, ever-blessed and most pure
and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the cherubim and more glorious
beyond compare than the seraphim, without corruption thou gavest birth to God
the Word: true Theotokos, we magnify thee.

label: The priest prays:

For the holy Prophet, Forerunner, and Baptist John; for the holy, glorious,
and all-laudable apostles, for Saint {N.}, whose memory we keep this day, and
for all thy saints, at whose supplications, visit us, O God.

note: [1] For feasts, see pp. 302ff. for the appropriate megalynarion.
